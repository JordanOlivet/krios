## Yield (ADR-038, pull request #13, merged)

- **One rule**: an operation gives the recipe's count, +25% per counted
  Yield point; a machine counts at most **2 Yield points per effective
  tier** (2/4/6/8), every machine that takes Yield included (the Death
  Field's Looting too). The grinder's tier multiplier is gone.
- **The bay refuses** a Yield module past the cap; modules already past it
  stay, inert. The stats line shows the points counted over the cap
  (`+50 % · 2/2`), amber while some sit idle. The user asked for this
  display during the grilling.
- **The Plasma Forge** smelts one for one and refuses Yield modules.
- **Metal ores grind straight into dusts** (iron, gold, neurite 2, copper
  7). A grinding result may be a list, first entry with an item wins: a
  modded ore names its dust, then its raw metal.
- **Ancient debris** grinds into 2 netherite scraps; **off the network**
  the grinder gives exactly half.
- No overflow mechanism: the largest operation is 39 lapis at the last tier.
- The reasoning, with the numbers before and after, is in
  `docs/analysis/yield-balance.md` (user request).
- A terminal's readout carries its stats rows (`krios.stats.<row>`), so the
  client `screens` scenario checks the yield line.

## The Magma Reactor (ADR-039, pull request #14, merged)

The first line born at tier 2, after a grilling session with the user
(design in `docs/mods/01-industry-design.md`, "Magma Reactor").

- **Lava only, twice the energy**: 640 E/mB, 640,000 E a bucket; 32 E/t
  base, 64 E/t at tier 2 (off the park ratio on purpose); 200,000 E and a
  16-bucket tank at tier 2. The Thermal Reactor keeps burning lava.
- **Lava in**: buckets (empty ones to an output slot), a lava cauldron on a
  manual FLUID-in face (the dripstone farm), other mods' pipes through the
  fluid capabilities. Lava out only through manual FLUID-out faces.
- **Energy capped at one point per effective tier**, every generator. Four
  module slots on the Magma Reactor.
- **Bug found and fixed on the way**: off the network, machines pulled
  energy from a touching Thermal Reactor only, so a full Magma Reactor
  never woke. They now pull from any `ContactEnergySource`.
- Texture drafts on the Thermal chassis with a lava core, new files only:
  `magma_reactor_front`, `_front_lit` (3 frames), `_side`, `_top`.

## A click on a panel keeps the stack in hand (pull request #15)

Found by the user in the play test: a click on a terminal's side panel
that missed a slot threw the carried stack to the ground, as vanilla does
outside the frame. `KriosTerminalScreen.hasClickedOutside` now answers no
inside `extraAreas()` (the annex panels, the open Codex), every terminal.

- Test: each open annex panel names a blank point, `panel.<name>.body`;
  the client tool gained `#expect carried`. The screens scenario picks up
  the Magma Reactor's empty bucket, presses `panel.stats.body`, and expects
  the bucket still on the cursor. Without the fix exactly that line failed.
