The numbers pass the previous session left open: counts and the stack at

## Yield (ADR-038, pull request #13)

- **One rule**: an operation gives the recipe's count, +25% per counted
  Yield point; a machine counts at most **2 Yield points per effective
  tier** (2/4/6/8), every machine that takes Yield included (the Death
  Field's Looting too). The grinder's tier multiplier is gone.
- **The bay refuses** a Yield module past the cap; modules already past it
  stay, inert. The stats line shows the points counted over the cap
  (`+50 % · 2/2`), amber while some sit idle. The user asked for this
  display during the grilling.
- **The Plasma Forge** smelts one for one and refuses Yield modules.
- **Metal ores grind straight into dusts** (iron, gold, neurite 2, copper
  7). A grinding result may be a list, first entry with an item wins: a
  modded ore names its dust, then its raw metal.
- **Ancient debris** grinds into 2 netherite scraps; **off the network**
  the grinder gives exactly half.
- No overflow mechanism: the largest operation is 39 lapis at the last tier.
- The reasoning, with the numbers before and after, is in
  `docs/analysis/yield-balance.md` (user request).
- A terminal's readout carries its stats rows (`krios.stats.<row>`), so the
  client `screens` scenario checks the yield line.
